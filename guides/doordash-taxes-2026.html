How much should DoorDash drivers save for taxes in 2026?

DoorDash doesn't withhold any tax from your pay. Here's how much to set aside each week, with examples.

Short answer: many part-time Dashers who log their miles and tips owe roughly 7% to 10% of gross earnings in federal tax for 2026. Without a mileage log, it can be close to double. Add your state tax on top.

What goes into the number

Self-employment tax: 15.3% on 92.35% of your profit (Social Security and Medicare). Nobody withholds it for you. IRS Topic 554

Mileage deduction: every business mile is worth 72.5¢ in 2026. 400 miles a week over 48 weeks is a $13,920 deduction. IRS 2026 rate

No tax on tips: delivery workers can deduct qualified tips from income tax, up to $25,000 a year from 2025 through 2028. How it works

20% business income deduction: the qualified business income deduction lowers income tax on your profit, and it's now permanent.

When to pay the IRS

If you expect to owe $1,000 or more, the IRS expects estimated payments on April 15, June 15, September 15 and January 15. If you also have a paycheck job, you can skip these by adding extra withholding on a new Form W-4 (Step 4c). Form 1040-ES
