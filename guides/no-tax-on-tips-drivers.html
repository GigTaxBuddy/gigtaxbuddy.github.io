No tax on tips for delivery and rideshare drivers

A new deduction lets drivers take tips off their income tax from 2025 through 2028. It only helps if you track your tips.

Short answer: rideshare drivers and delivery workers are on the Treasury's list of tipped occupations. Self-employed drivers can deduct qualified tips, up to $25,000 a year, from income tax. It doesn't reduce self-employment tax.

Who qualifies

The Treasury list includes Taxi and Rideshare Drivers and Chauffeurs (occupation 802) and Goods Delivery People (804), which covers food, grocery and package delivery. Treasury occupation list

How much can it save?

Example: single filer with a $40,000 day job, $25,000 in gig earnings including $6,000 in tips, and 15,000 business miles. Deducting the tips lowers federal tax by about $720 in 2026.

How to track tips

Each app shows tips separately in your earnings history. Log the tip amount with each shift or each week. Gig Tax Buddy has a tips field and applies the deduction for you, then shows the effect on your weekly set-aside.
